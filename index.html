About Yukti Rail

SIH Prototype

AI-assisted integrated block planning prototype for Indian Railways

The Problem

Engineering, Traction Distribution and Signalling departments may require maintenance work on the same railway section. If planned separately, repeated blocks can affect train operations.

Our Solution

Yukti Rail identifies compatible Engineering, TRD and S&T requests and recommends one integrated block for officer review.

How It Works

1 Collect maintenance requests
2 Prioritize safety and urgency
3 Recommend integrated blocks
4 Railway officials review and approve

Key Features

Integrated Block Recommendation Explainable Priority Score Corridor Visualization What-If Simulation Human Approval Workflow Past Data Analytics

Prototype Disclaimer

This is a Smart India Hackathon frontend prototype using synthetic demonstration data. It is not connected to live Indian Railways systems and does not autonomously approve, grant or execute railway blocks.
